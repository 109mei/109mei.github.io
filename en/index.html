    About

    I am a graduate student at the Graduate School of Kyushu Institute of Information Sciences, in the Arahira Laboratory. My research is on designing and running educational game jams for beginners — how short, structured game-making events can teach programming to people who have never written code.

    I also lead IDERIA, a student group that makes games and teaching materials with an educational purpose (memory training, relaxation through ASMR, and so on), and runs VR and programming workshops for school students.

    Education: Fukuoka Prefectural Kaho Sogo High School → Kyushu Institute of Information Sciences → its Graduate School (Arahira Lab).
